# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Assume good intent, focus on technical
issues, and keep discussions welcoming for contributors of all experience
levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, trolling, publishing
private information, or other unprofessional conduct is not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers will
review reports confidentially and take appropriate action.
